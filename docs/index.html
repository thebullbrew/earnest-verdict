The Earnest
Verdict

Earnest money is not a tip for the seller. It is cash you hand over to prove you are serious — and cash you can lose if the deal dies after your contingencies expire. Too little and you lose the house. Too much and you lose your leverage. This tool finds the smallest deposit that makes you competitive without overexposing you.

1 · The offer

Rule of thumb being tested here: earnest only helps until it matches the seller's fear you will walk. Past that, extra dollars buy almost no extra credibility — they only buy you extra loss if you do walk.

2 · Why this verdict

Competitiveness is scored against the local norm: matching the norm scores about 50, each full point above it adds credibility up to a cap, and a crowded field raises the bar. Risk is priced two ways — the dollars themselves, and what share of the cash you will still need after closing they consume. Waived contingencies do not make a big deposit braver. They make it more exposed, sooner.
